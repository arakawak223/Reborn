Injury Details

Limb deficiencies in all four extremities. Compounded by years of shoulder strain from competition and the frustration of a 13-year gold medal drought.

Right shoulder rotator cuff tear

Tore the right shoulder rotator cuff during a throwing motion. Career-threatening injury — 2018 / 12 months recovery

Golden Quotes

"Instead of searching for what I'm missing, I think about how to use what I have. I may be missing limbs, but in the water, I'm free. Water doesn't treat me as disabled."

"I went thirteen years without a gold medal. That time is what made me a real athlete. From Beijing onward, I thought about quitting countless times. But I moved to England, rebuilt my stroke from the ground up, and the result was gold in Tokyo and Paris."

\u2014 After winning gold at the Tokyo Paralympics

Testimonies
