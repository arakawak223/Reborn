The Comeback Story

The comeback of Sena Irie, told in 5 chapters

01

Origin–2020

The Boxer Born of Frogs and Manga

Yonago, Tottori Prefecture. Sena Irie was drawn to boxing by the manga "Ganbare Genki." Captivated by its hero, who fights the world with nothing but his fists, she walked into a local gym as an elementary school student.

Her other great passion was frogs. She kept frogs at home and made it a daily ritual to observe their behavior. "A frog stays perfectly still, storing energy until the moment it leaps. I want to be the same way." This distinctive sensibility shaped her boxing style as well.

She loved repetitive drills more than anyone, throwing the same punch thousands of times over. Not flashy, but precise — her boxing reliably found the opponent's vital points. She enrolled at Nippon Sport Science University and made her name in the amateur boxing world.

02

Despair2020

Losing Streaks and a Slump — Days When Retirement Crossed Her Mind

As the Tokyo Olympic selection drew near, Irie fell into a severe slump. Losses piled up, and the precision punches she was known for vanished. The suspicion that her boxing simply was not good enough crept in, making her feet heavy every time she headed to the gym.

"I have no talent." She had known it since childhood, and now that knowledge gnawed at her in the worst possible way. She had always compensated for a lack of talent with practice. But when even practice stopped producing results, what was left to believe in?

For a time, she seriously considered retirement. "Maybe I should quit boxing and become a frog researcher." She was half in earnest when the thought crossed her mind.

03

The Void2020–2021

What the Frogs Taught Her About the Art of Waiting

At the bottom of her slump, Irie spent her days holed up at home, watching her frogs. The frog in the aquarium always sank down quietly before it leaped. It stored its energy, then poured everything into a single explosive jump.

"Frogs never rush. They stay still, storing energy until the very moment they leap. All I have to do is the same." The frog's way of life taught her the meaning of waiting.

Her mind settled, Irie overhauled her boxing from the ground up. She reviewed film with her coach, adjusting her reaction speed to opponents' movements, her punching angles, and her footwork one element at a time. "Store, then leap" — simple, but essential. She made that fighting philosophy her own once more. The unwavering support of her family quietly bolstered this process of reconstruction.

04

AwakeningSummer 2021

Throwing Punches on the Olympic Stage

The featherweight boxing division at the 2021 Tokyo Olympics. Sena Irie advanced methodically from the opening round to the final. There were no flashy knockouts. But in every bout, she calmly read her opponent's attacks and kept landing precise counters.

The "reaction" and "precision" she had reforged during her slump came into full bloom on the grandest stage of all. "The bitterness of losing is the finest essence for the next victory." True to those words, every loss she had endured during the slump became nourishment for her Olympic campaign.

The final. Against a formidable Filipino opponent, Irie fought with a blend of composure and daring to claim the victory.

05

RebirthSummer 2021

The First Olympic Gold Medal in Japanese Women's Boxing History

The first Olympic gold medal in the history of Japanese women's boxing. It was won by a girl from Tottori Prefecture who always said, "I have no talent."

"Her strength is deceptive — she looks timid, but there is an inner core that absolutely will not break." (Her coach)

She began boxing because of the manga "Ganbare Genki." She learned a philosophy of life from frogs. She clawed her way out of the despair of a slump and stood atop the Olympic podium. The story of Sena Irie is the exhilarating tale of a person who believed she had no talent conquering the world with the only weapon she possessed: effort.

With the gold medal around her neck, the first thing Irie reached for was a stuffed frog. "I stored my energy and leapt — just like a frog." Her smile in that moment shone brighter than anyone's.
