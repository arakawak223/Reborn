The Comeback Story

The comeback of Roki Sasaki, told in 5 chapters

01

Origin2011–2019

The Boy Who Survived the Tsunami

March 11, 2011. The Great East Japan Earthquake. Nine-year-old Roki Sasaki, living in Rikuzentakata, Iwate Prefecture, lost his father and grandparents to the tsunami. His home was swept away. The town he had known vanished without a trace, and overnight the boy lost everything.

Life in an evacuation shelter. His mother, Yoko, resolved to raise her three sons alone. Roki kept his grief hidden, silently continuing to play baseball. The feel of the ball in his hand was the only thing anchoring him to reality.

When he enrolled at Ofunato High School, his talent erupted. A blazing fastball topping 163 km/h——far beyond the realm of a high school player. Yet coach Kokubo deliberately chose not to put Roki on the mound for the prefectural tournament final. "For the sake of his future"——the decision sparked fierce debate, but time would prove it right.

02

Despair2020–2021

"A Glass Figurine"——The Monster Who Couldn't Pitch

After turning professional, Roki Sasaki struggled. The bone fragility of a still-growing body, the tightness in his right arm. His young frame could not keep pace with the monstrous power of a 165 km/h fastball.

In his first year, the team deliberately kept him off the first-team roster. A professional baseball player who never pitched in an official game——for Roki, it was an ordeal beyond anything he had imagined. Watching his fellow rookies perform on television, he felt his body was nothing more than a "glass figurine."

The impulse to pitch warred with the terror of breaking apart. Caught between the two, the boy grew into a man. Whenever he recalled his father, lost in the earthquake, he told himself: "Pitching is living."

03

The Void2021–2022

The Preparation Period——Rebuilding His Body

"The time I couldn't pitch was a preparation period for my body to grow bigger and stronger."

Under the team's meticulous oversight, Roki carefully strengthened his body. His training regimen was managed scientifically, with pitching intervals, pitch counts, and conditioning monitored down to the finest detail.

Manager Yoshii reflected: "His talent is a national treasure. The fact that we developed him without rushing is what created this untouchable state." Not rushing——that was the hardest thing for a boy who had survived a tsunami. Yet his mother's support and his manager's trust taught him the "courage to wait."

During that period, Roki threw himself into dietary management and physical transformation. He added muscle to his once-slender frame, building the endurance to fire 165 km/h fastballs for dozens of pitches at a time. The glass figurine was being reborn as a body of steel.

04

AwakeningApril 2022

The Perfect Game——A World Record of 13 Consecutive Strikeouts

April 10, 2022. Lotte versus Orix. Roki Sasaki accomplished a feat that will be etched into professional baseball history for eternity. A perfect game. And that was not all: 13 consecutive strikeouts——a world record. His 19 total strikeouts tied the Japanese record as well.

A 165 km/h fastball and a forkball in the 140 km/h range. Batters could do nothing but watch the ball sail past or swing at air. Of 28 outs recorded, 19 were strikeouts. This was no longer human pitching——it was art.

After the game, Roki said: "Baseball is my way of repaying everyone who supported me." The boy who lost everything in the earthquake learned to wait, rebuilt his body, and rewrote Japanese baseball history.

At the 2023 World Baseball Classic, he helped Japan claim the championship. As he overpowered the world's best hitters, his mother back in Rikuzentakata wept.

05

Rebirth2023–

Pitching as Gratitude——To the World Stage

After the perfect game and the WBC championship, Roki Sasaki became one of Japan's foremost pitchers. Yet his gaze always faces two directions at once: one toward the future of baseball, the other toward the past of Rikuzentakata.

Every year on March 11, he honors the date, participating in efforts to pass on the memory of the disaster. He believes that excelling at baseball is the greatest tribute he can offer to his late father and grandparents.

"His talent is a national treasure. The fact that we developed him without rushing is what created this untouchable state."——True to Manager Yoshii's words, the monster nurtured with patience has grown into a world-class pitcher.

The boy who lost everything to the tsunami clutched the light of baseball and transformed a "glass figurine" into "the fastest arm in the world." Roki Sasaki's every pitch is an act of gratitude, a beacon of hope, and the very essence of being alive.
