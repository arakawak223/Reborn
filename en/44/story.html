The Comeback Story

The comeback of Hikaru Oe, told in 5 chapters

01

Origin–2015

A Technician Raised by Snowy Mountains

Toyama City, Toyama Prefecture. Born in one of Japan's heaviest snowfall regions along the Sea of Japan coast, Hikaru Oe grew up with the mountains as her playground. She picked up skiing and snowboarding naturally, and in the halfpipe she displayed exceptional technique.

Soft, fluid board handling and graceful body control in the air. Her aerials were hailed as "artistic," and she won a gold medal at the Youth Olympics. She also placed at the X Games, and was expected to carry the future of Japanese women's halfpipe on her shoulders.

Born among snowy peaks, raised by snowy peaks. For her, snowboarding was play, expression, and life itself.

02

Despair2016–2018

Torn ACL in Both Knees——The Worst Possible Injury

For a snowboarder, a torn anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) is the worst injury imaginable. If the knee that absorbs the shock of landing breaks down, aerial tricks become impossible. Oe suffered this worst-case injury——in both knees.

First, her right knee. A botched landing in competition, and the knee buckled. Surgery and rehabilitation followed, and she made her comeback——only for her left knee to give way. Devastated by the repeated ACL tears, she was plunged into despair.

"Your knees can't take any more." "You should give up snowboarding."——Doctors and those around her all said the same. For a woman in her twenties with destroyed ligaments in both knees, a future in a sport that demands hurtling down a halfpipe at high speed and spinning through the air seemed impossible.

03

The Void2018–2020

Reassembling Her Muscles Like a Puzzle

"Rehabilitation was like reassembling my own muscles piece by piece, like a puzzle."

Multiple surgeries and a combined rehabilitation period spanning more than two years. During her time away from the mountains, Oe confronted her body with ruthless honesty. She rebuilt each muscle supporting her knees one by one, strengthened her core, and analyzed her landing mechanics in slow motion hundreds of times.

"The loneliness of not being able to ride the board was greater than the fear of riding it."

Fear was real. Her knees might shatter again. But every time she looked up at the snow-capped mountains, the urge to ride overwhelmed the terror. The support of her coach and family illuminated the dark days of her struggle.

Through the rehabilitation process, she mastered a new form that minimized the load on her knees during landings. Her landings became softer and more stable than they had ever been before the injuries. When the shattered puzzle was reassembled, the picture that emerged was more beautiful than the original.

04

Awakening2021

Soaring Through the Sky Once More

The day of her return. For the first time in over two years, Oe crested the lip of the halfpipe and launched into the sky. Both knees were wrapped in braces, and her heart hammered with fear.

But at the moment of landing, her knees held firm. The muscles forged in rehabilitation and her new form supported her knees solidly. The tears would not stop.

"Everyone in the snowboarding community said the same thing: 'Her knees should be wrecked, but her riding is softer and stronger than before.'"

When Oe returned to competition, she displayed a style unlike anything she had shown before. No longer relying on brute force for her aerials, she demonstrated supple, beautiful tricks with impeccable control over her body's axis. The injury had not destroyed her——it had "remade" her.

05

Rebirth2022–

Reaching the World Stage on Battered Knees

Hikaru Oe once again stands on the world stage. With a body that has endured torn ACLs in both knees and multiple surgeries, she continues to soar through breathtaking aerials.

"The loneliness of not being able to ride the board was greater than the fear of riding it"——those words say everything. What conquered her fear was not courage alone, but her love for snowboarding.

The core strength gained through rehabilitation, the improved landing form, the maturation of her mental game. Growing stronger each time she broke, her journey has given "battered knees" a new meaning. They are not a symbol of weakness, but proof that she stood back up, again and again.

The technician raised among Toyama's snowy mountains rides the sky today, accompanied by the ache in both knees——a new self, built from the pieces of a shattered puzzle.
