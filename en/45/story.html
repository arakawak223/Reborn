The Comeback Story

The comeback of Reia Taniguchi, told in 5 chapters

01

OriginChildhood–

A Girl Who Loved the Snow

Born in Shiga Prefecture, Rea Taniguchi became obsessed with snowboarding every winter from a young age. She frequented ski resorts across the Kansai region, learning the joy of riding through her body.

Better than her peers, and bolder. The sight of her gliding freely down the slopes was like a snow fairy. Snowboarding was not merely a sport——it was her very identity.

She was born with a congenital impairment in her left foot, but to her it was simply "the way things were." Even though her left foot did not function fully, it was her body, and that was that. She adapted her riding in her own way, forging a style uniquely her own.

02

DespairAt the time of the injury

A Devastating Injury in Competition

On top of her congenital disability, a serious injury sustained during competition temporarily pulled Taniguchi away from snowboarding. The impact of a fall concentrated on her impaired left foot, causing further damage.

Surgery was required. Her prosthetic and brace needed adjustment. The days without the mountains stretched on, and every glimpse of falling snow through the window tightened the ache in her chest.

"If you fall and get back up, it's not a failure"——she repeated those words to herself like a mantra, yet the reality of a body that could not rise tormented her. A congenital disability compounded by an acquired injury——a double burden. Even so, she never let go of her longing for the mountains.

03

The VoidRehabilitation period

Adjusting Prosthetics and Braces——Rebuilding Her Body

After surgery, the process of adjusting her prosthetic and brace began. A prosthetic optimized for snowboarding is entirely different from one designed for daily life. Together with her prosthetist, she rebuilt it over and over——a prosthetic capable of absorbing the shock from the snow's surface and transmitting the subtle forces needed to control the board.

"My disability is just one part of who I am. On the board, we're all the same."

Her encounter with rider Keiji Okamoto proved a major turning point. From the pioneer of para-snowboarding, she learned not only the technique of riding with a disability, but also the mindset of transforming that disability into a "weapon uniquely her own."

Rehabilitation was grueling. It took months before she could stand on the snow again. But the emotion of strapping on a board once more swept away all the suffering. "Ah——I really am someone who was born to ride," she thought.

04

AwakeningNational team candidacy

Discovering "Her Own Style of Riding"

The riding Taniguchi displayed after her return was unlike that of any able-bodied snowboarder or any other para-snowboarder——a style utterly her own. A body that had overcome both a congenital disability in her left foot and an acquired injury produced a unique balance and weight distribution all its own.

"Her rate of improvement is astonishing. She has a talent for embracing adversity."——So the national team staff assessed her.

She began posting results in domestic competitions, and her name appeared among the candidates for the Japanese para-snowboard team. The woman who had once felt limited "because of her disability" now took pride in it as an integral part of her identity.

Fall down and get back up. Again and again. That relentless cycle guided her down the road to Beijing.

05

Rebirth2022

The Beijing Paralympics——On the Board, We're All the Same

The 2022 Beijing Paralympics. Rea Taniguchi stood on the dream stage as a member of Japan's para-snowboard team. She had overcome a congenital disability and a devastating competition injury to arrive here.

"My disability is just one part of who I am. On the board, we're all the same"——true to those words, she showcased a ride that was unmistakably hers. More than any result, the mere fact of standing there was her victory.

The spirit of "turning disability into a weapon," inherited from Keiji Okamoto, will be passed on to the next generation. Taniguchi's achievements have given hope to young snowboarders who share her disability.

"If you fall and get back up, it's not a failure"——the girl who loved the snow fell countless times, rose countless times, and reached the world stage. Her story is a beautiful testament to the truth that "disability" and "possibility" can coexist.
