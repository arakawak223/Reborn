The Comeback Story

The comeback of Wataru Endo, told in 5 chapters

01

Origin–2022

The Analysis Notebook That Forged a Duel King

Yokohama, Kanagawa Prefecture. From an early age, Wataru aspired to play overseas. Beginning in middle school, he kept a self-made "analysis notebook." Opponents' movements, areas for improvement in his own play, tactical ideas — he recorded everything and ran his own improvement cycle.

After launching his professional career at Shonan Bellmare, he moved to Urawa Red Diamonds, then to Belgium, and on to VfB Stuttgart in Germany's Bundesliga. In the Bundesliga, he earned the title of "Duel King" — highest win rate in one-on-one battles — twice. His tenacity in challenges and tactical intelligence allowed him to outperform Germany's most physically imposing players.

He was entrusted with the captaincy of the Japan national team, serving as the team's heartbeat. At the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar, he was a central figure in Japan's historic victories over Germany and Spain.

02

DespairAutumn 2022

Concussion Just Before the World Cup — His Participation in Doubt

Just before the opening of the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar, Wataru suffered a concussion. In football, a concussion is a serious injury that can threaten a player's career with a single impact.

Headaches, dizziness, difficulty concentrating. Doctors recommended a sufficient recovery period. But the World Cup would not wait. The possibility that Japan's captain might be sidelined on the eve of the most important tournament spread anxiety throughout the entire squad.

On top of that, competition for his position at his club had intensified. There were stretches where he was relegated to the bench, and he had to fight the fear of losing his match sharpness. For a man who declared that "preparation is everything," being unable to prepare was the gravest crisis imaginable.

03

The VoidAutumn 2022

The Days He Burned Video into His Brain

While waiting for his concussion to heal, Wataru devoted every moment off the pitch to "brain training." He watched video footage of opposing players for hours on end, over and over again.

The habits of each German national team player, their passing tendencies, their defensive positioning. The patterns of Spain's tiki-taka attack and the spaces that could become vulnerabilities. He drilled all of it into his brain.

"The time I spent on the bench made me tougher." The experience of watching matches from the sideline taught him the full picture of the game — something he couldn't see while on the pitch. His rehabilitation was a joint effort with the national team doctor. His family's support sustained him. The smiles of his four children became the driving force behind his desire to recover quickly.

"Preparation is everything. The outcome is decided before the match begins." True to his words, the time he spent unable to take the field became the greatest period of "preparation" in his life.

04

AwakeningWinter 2022

The Architect of Germany and Spain's Defeat

Having recovered with remarkable speed in time for the tournament, Wataru unleashed the full fruits of his preparation on the pitch.

Group stage, first match: versus Germany. From the base of midfield, Wataru snuffed out German attacks one after another. Every movement of the opposition, studied exhaustively on video, unfolded exactly "as predicted." Japan defeated Germany 2-1. The world was stunned.

Then, the third match: versus Spain. Once again, Wataru dominated the midfield, disrupting Spain's passing networks. Japan came from behind to win yet again. Defeating both Germany and Spain in the same group — a feat for the ages in football history.

"He's the midfield sweeper. His fearless play gives courage to everyone around him." That assessment was accurate — Wataru's contributions rarely showed up in the statistics, but he was the "heart" that sustained the entire team. The man who had been pushed to the brink by a concussion shone brightest at the World Cup.

05

Rebirth2023–

To Liverpool — The Boy with the Notebook Reaches the Summit

His World Cup heroics earned global recognition, and in 2023, Wataru transferred to Liverpool FC, one of the most prestigious clubs in the English Premier League. A Japanese player wearing the Liverpool shirt — what had once been a fantasy became reality.

Under manager Jurgen Klopp, Wataru was deployed as the holding midfielder. "Wataru is the heart of my team." Coming from one of the world's most acclaimed coaches, those words spoke to the magnitude of Wataru's presence within the squad.

The boy from Yokohama who started keeping an analysis notebook in middle school overcame a pre-tournament concussion, helped topple Germany and Spain, and earned his place at a Premier League giant. The time on the bench, the concussion that threatened his participation — every setback was fuel that made him "tougher."

"Preparation is everything." The story of the man who practiced that creed with unyielding sincerity is proof that it is not "flashy play" but "diligent preparation" that changes the world. Wataru Endo opens his notebook today, already preparing for tomorrow's match.
